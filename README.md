# ANU CSSA Game Jam


### Core mechanic
**The Aussie PM is escaping parliament house… THE PEOPLE HAVE REVOLTED!**

### Prototype development cycle
* Determine shared workflow
* Determine role allocation
### Additional features - beyond the prototype

* Add detail by hand
* Add detail with assets
* Character selection menu
* More advanced room design
* Popups instead of passive ‘chat’ demonstrating power
* Animate the still
* Replace office for senate
### Paradigms
* 2 - dimensional
* Top down
* Closed world
* Room-oriented
* Procedural generation
* Continuous interaction
* Continuous movement
### Australian References
* Vegemite
* ANU References
* Clive palmer
* Pauline Hansen

### Key first steps for prototype
* Develop basic movement
* Basic rooms

### Timeline of Gameplay

* Opens with still of parliament house
* Player has core mechanics explained in title crawl
* Beginning still at the office of parliament house
* People are upset at player, chase them and their ‘damage’ reflects a loss in power
* Player moves out of office and into the smaller rooms on the way out
* Ends outside parliament house
* Ending still is determined based on performance
